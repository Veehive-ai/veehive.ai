Messaging

What we say

Not new writing — the lines the site and the card already carry, pinned so they stop drifting. Veehive currently describes itself three ways across four surfaces: agentic apps on the site and the card, AI products in the link preview, AI agents in the email signature. One of them has to win. Click any line to copy it.

The nounOn the site, the card and the CTA already. Specific where “AI products” could be anyone, and it names what someone buys rather than what is inside it.
The lineThe homepage h1, the card tagline and the link-preview title. Three surfaces already agree — this is the one that has earned it.
The shapeFrom the back of the card. The shortest true statement of the architecture, and it heads off the six-versus-seven question before it is asked.

What we don’t say

Not these words

  • AI products — true of anyone. Agentic apps is ours.
  • AI agents as the headline noun — it describes the parts, not what someone buys.
  • Solutions, platform, cutting-edge, revolutionary.
  • Veehive AI or VeeHive — see the naming table.

Not these claims

  • Replacing people. The apps are shaped around how a business already runs.
  • Seven apps. Six apps and a harness.
  • Anything about model providers or benchmarks — we sell the outcome.
  • Certification beyond what we hold: Dubai AI Seal, Tier E.

The mark

Six petals, six apps

Each petal carries one product colour, in a fixed arrangement. The mark can stand alone wherever the name is already present — app icons, avatars, favicons.

Six petals, six apps — and seven product marks. HiveFront is the seventh: it is not a seventh app but the harness the other six run on, so it has a mark and a colour of its own without a petal. That pink is also the logotype’s one accent.

Clear space. Keep free space on all sides equal to the height of one petal. Nothing sits inside it.

Minimum size. 24 px for the mark, 120 px wide for the full logotype. Below that the petal gaps close up.

Motion

The mark in motion

The mark is radially symmetric, so it rotates about its own centre without ever looking tilted — which makes it the loading state everywhere. Six timings, pure CSS, no JavaScript. Use the default unless there is a reason not to.

Typography

Manrope, IBM Plex Sans and Mono

Manrope draws the logotype and every heading. IBM Plex Sans carries body copy, interface text and long-form writing. IBM Plex Mono sets the small uppercase labels — eyebrows, captions, hex codes, file paths — and is the reason they hold their rhythm. All three are open source under the SIL Open Font License and ship in the kit. This page pulls them from Google Fonts, but anything built from the kit’s own files needs the mono too — it was the one face named in the spec and missing from the folder.

Merch

On cloth

Three placements. The full logotype sits centred on the chest, and small — it is a shirt, not a billboard. The mark alone sits at the left chest (the wearer’s left, so the right as you look at it), about a hand’s width down from the shoulder seam. And the mascot sits above the wordmark where the piece is meant to be fun rather than corporate. Print the white logotype on anything darker than the mid-grey, the dark one on anything lighter — never a drop shadow, never an outline to make it work on a difficult colour.

Email signature

One signature, every inbox

Tables and inline styles, because that is still what mail clients want. The logo loads from veehive.ai so it survives the trip into someone else’s inbox — never paste a screenshot of it, and never attach it. The pink rule is HiveFront’s, the one accent the signature carries.

Using it

Rules that matter

✓Do

  • Use the supplied SVG wherever it will scale.
  • Pick the version that matches the ground: dark wordmark on light, white on dark.
  • Keep one petal-height of clear space around the mark.
  • Let the mark stand alone when the name is already on the page.

×Don't

  • Recolour, rotate or redraw the petals, or change their arrangement.
  • Move the pink dot, or add pink anywhere else in the logotype.
  • Stretch, shear or outline the logotype, or add a shadow to it.
  • Set the wordmark in another typeface, or retype it instead of using the file.
  • Place the full-colour mark on a busy photograph without a clear plate behind it.
