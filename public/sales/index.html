Honest gaps

What this kit does not have yet

Listed rather than quietly missing, so nobody promises a buyer something that does not exist.

Not built

  • A pitch deck. No slide template exists yet; decks are being built from scratch each time, which is how wording drifts.
  • A price list. Nothing published. Pricing conversations are bespoke today — do not quote a figure from memory.
  • Battle cards. No written comparison against named competitors.
  • A proposal template. The card and leaflet are the only print pieces.
  • Written case-study PDFs. The five above are one-paragraph entries, not leave-behinds.
