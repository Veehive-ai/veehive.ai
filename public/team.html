Veehive was founded by Sathish, who serves as the Founder & CEO. The leadership team includes Taj (Delivery & COO), Dixon (Product & CGO), Chakir (Head of Technology), and Fahim (Head of Engineering). The team also includes Nitesh (AI Engineer MCP).
Veehive builds an agentic workforce of five specialized AI agents: SnapaPro (AI project management with natural-language intake), mpliphi (customer-operations agent handling 50K+ conversations/month across WhatsApp and email), Studio (content-production agent turning documents into branded video), HiveLink (video-first community platform — one branded destination for video, conversation, events, and CRM), and Veehive Mind (always-on campaign hive of nine specialist agents that launch and run marketing campaigns from a single brief).
You describe what you want in plain English. SnapaPro turns it into a campaign plan. Studio creates Arabic + English video variants automatically. HiveLink distributes across your channels and centralizes approvals before publishing. mpliphi automates high-volume inbound responses. One prompt, four modules — reduce time-to-launch from weeks to hours.
Most marketing teams use 7+ separate tools for planning, content creation, distribution, and engagement. Veehive replaces all of them with one agentic workforce. You give one command, and four integrated modules handle the entire campaign — from plan to published, with customer engagement on autopilot.
